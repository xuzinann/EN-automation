# Project Brief: TechServ Solutions — Acquisition Due Diligence

## Deal Overview

Apex Capital Partners is evaluating the acquisition of TechServ Solutions, a mid-market IT services and managed infrastructure company headquartered in Chicago, IL. The target has been operating for 12 years and serves mid-market enterprise clients primarily in financial services and healthcare.

## Target Company Profile

- **Company:** TechServ Solutions
- **Founded:** 2014
- **HQ:** Chicago, IL (with satellite offices in Dallas, TX and Atlanta, GA)
- **Estimated Revenue:** $170-200M (per broker CIM)
- **Estimated Headcount:** ~620 employees (per LinkedIn data as of Q1 2026)
- **Core Services:** Managed IT infrastructure, cloud migration, cybersecurity consulting, 24/7 help desk
- **Key Clients:** 3 regional banks, 2 hospital systems, several mid-market manufacturers

## Investment Thesis

Apex believes TechServ is operationally efficient with strong recurring revenue from managed services contracts (estimated 65-70% recurring). The platform could serve as a base for a roll-up strategy in the fragmented IT services space.

## Key Hypotheses to Test

1. **Headcount Efficiency:** TechServ operates with a lean team relative to revenue — need to verify actual headcount by role (engineering vs. sales vs. operations vs. management)
2. **Revenue Quality:** Majority of revenue is recurring via multi-year managed services contracts
3. **Key Person Risk:** CTO James Morrison and VP Engineering Sarah Chen are believed to be critical to service delivery — need to assess organizational depth below them
4. **Client Concentration:** Top 3 clients may represent >40% of revenue
5. **Technology Moat:** Proprietary monitoring platform "ServeWatch" may provide competitive differentiation
6. **Attrition:** Industry average for IT services is 18-22% annual attrition — need to verify TechServ's actual numbers

## Known Data Points

- LinkedIn shows 620 employees, but contractor usage is unknown
- Glassdoor reviews mention "long hours but good culture" — 3.8/5 rating
- CIM states $185M revenue for FY2025
- Company won "Best IT Services Provider" from Chicago Business Journal in 2024
- James Morrison (CTO) has been with the company since founding
- Sarah Chen (VP Eng) joined 3 years ago from Accenture
- The company recently opened the Atlanta office (2025)

## Gaps to Fill via Expert Calls

1. Actual engineering headcount vs. contractors (LinkedIn may overcount)
2. Organizational structure below C-suite — how many layers? Who are the key managers?
3. Real attrition rates in engineering and help desk over last 24 months
4. Depth of "ServeWatch" platform — is it truly proprietary or a wrapper around open-source tools?
5. Client contract structure — length, renewal rates, pricing power
6. Sales pipeline health and new client acquisition strategy
7. Integration complexity — how hard would it be to merge with another IT services firm?

## Expert Profile Needed

Former VP Engineering, Director of Operations, or Senior Technical Manager at TechServ Solutions who departed within the last 6-18 months. Should have visibility into org structure, headcount, technology decisions, and operational metrics.
